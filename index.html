Frequently Asked Questions

What tools does Neuron replace?

Neuron replaces Calendly, Mailchimp, Squarespace, Canva Pro, Linktree, Manychat, Kajabi, Google Analytics, Circle and most other point-tools you're stitching together today. Everything lives in one workspace with a single inbox, single contact record, and single source of truth.

What type of business is Neuron for?

Neuron is for ambitious businesses that want to take back control of their marketing and leads, regardless of your industry. We have seen success with trades businesses, coaches, consultants, course providers, professional businesses (such as insurance brokers, lawyers, accountants etc), creative business owners and so many more.

What can Neuron integrate with?

Neuron is designed to integrate with so many things — Xero, Quickbooks, ServiceM8, Google, Zoom, Outlook, Stripe, PayPal, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and more. For everything we don't directly integrate with, you can generally connect via Zapier or Make.

What support is available to me once I join?

Our team has put together an extensive library of videos providing information on how to use each of the different features within the platform. Additionally, we offer a done-for-you service if that's something you'd prefer — just get in touch to learn more.
